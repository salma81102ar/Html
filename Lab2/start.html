*What is Linked in:*

LinkedIn is a global professional networking platform that connects
individuals and businesses for career development, networking, and recruitment.
LinkedIn is an online social network designed for professionals ,
launched in May 2003 by Reid Hoffman and Eric Ly in the United States ,
and later acquired by Microsoft in 2016 for $26.2 billion.
The platform allows users to create professional profiles similar to resumes,
showcasing their work experience, skills, education, and professional achievements.
As of 2026, LinkedIn has over 1.2 billion registered members across more than 200 countries.

Professional and Business Applications
LinkedIn serves as a platform for professional visibility and authority-building,
enabling entrepreneurs, executives, and employees to establish credibility in their industries.
It also provides tools for market research, networking, and talent acquisition,
making it a key resource for both individuals and organizations seeking growth and professional development.
